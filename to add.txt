⚔️ Adventure & dungeon
Biome depths: every 10 depths a new kind of world (frozen caverns, sunken ruins, clockwork city), each with its own hazards, monsters and music.
Endless mode after depth 50: escalating depths with leaderboard codes to share with your friend.
Events inside a floor: a monster stampede, a collapsing floor you must escape, a wandering merchant being chased, a "lights out" floor.
Elite monster affixes (frenzied, teleporting, splitting, shielding allies), which make champions more memorable.
Secret levels reached by solving a floor puzzle, with unique loot tables.
Rival adventurer: a named NPC hero who shows up every few depths, grows stronger alongside you, and is fought as a mini-boss.
🧙 Hero & gear
Class specializations at level 25, choosing one of two subclasses, e.g. Warrior → Berserker or Guardian.
Weapon skills: each weapon type (sword, axe, bow, staff…) unlocks a unique special move as you master it.
Transmog / wardrobe: keep a look while wearing better stats, which pairs nicely with the cosmetics you already have.
Legendary quest items: multi-step hunts for named weapons with lore, which could tie into the Lost Journal.
Pet abilities tree: pets learn one active trick each (fetch loot, taunt, heal) as they level.
Mount racing / time trials on the island.
🏘️ Town & home
Your house interior: furniture bought or crafted, with trophies from bosses on the walls and a display case for legendaries.
Seasonal town events beyond Halloween, such as a winter festival, a spring planting fair and a summer fishing derby, each with its own currency and rewards (like the Peddler).
Town reputation: the favors feed into a town rank that unlocks new NPCs, e.g. a cartographer or a jeweler.
Night market: after dark, a shady vendor sells cursed or risky items.
Expedition board: send spare pets or mounts on timed real-time missions for materials, using the same timer style as the farm.
🌾 Island life
Beekeeping & orchards: honey, wax and candles, plus crafting with them.
Mining cave on the island for ore and gems, with its own small mini-game.
Boat & fishing trips to small offshore islets with rare fish, treasure and a sea monster boss.
Animal care: grooming and petting for happiness bonuses, ribbons from a livestock show.
Cooking recipes book: discovered recipes become a collectible, and meals get star ratings.
👥 Friends & co-op
Co-op bosses with mechanics that need two players (two switches, split aggro).
Shared town visits: your friend can tour your island, farm and house.
Gift economy: mail seeds, fish and crafted goods, with a "friendship level" that unlocks perks.
Weekly challenge seeds you both race on, with results exchanged by code.
🎮 Polish & release-readiness (for open source)
Controller pass: full menu navigation with the D-pad, and button prompts that switch between keyboard and gamepad.
Save export/import: download and upload a save file, which matters for players switching browsers or machines.
Performance mode for older laptops: fewer particles, a lower-resolution first-person view.
Accessibility: remappable controller buttons, a dyslexia-friendly font option, screen-reader-friendly menus.
Credits & attributions screen, which an open-source release benefits from.
Splitting the single file into modules for the open-source repository, with a simple build step that still produces one HTML file. This would make it far easier for contributors.
Balance pass with telemetry-free playtest logs: an optional local log of deaths, damage taken and gold curves so you can tune difficulty.
Mobile/touch controls: a virtual joystick and tap-to-attack, so it plays on phones and tablets too.
🌟 My top picks
#28 Save export/import: essential before release, so players can't lose a long-lived family to a cleared browser.
#7 Class specializations: a big expansion of replay value on top of the new four-tier trees.
#14 Seasonal events: they keep the world feeling alive and fit the calendar system you already have.
#13 House interior: a satisfying home for trophies, since your family history matters so much in this game.
#32 Code split: it would make the open-source repository much friendlier to contributors.
